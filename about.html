About This Recommendation

Outfit Forecast turns a seven-day weather record into one quick clothing suggestion and only the reminders that apply.

Back to forecast
Created byZehan
01

Weather data

The prototype uses Open-Meteo for current conditions and a seven-day forecast. The displayed update time belongs to the selected weather record.

Forecasts are model-based estimates. Conditions and recommendations can change as newer data becomes available.

02

How recommendations work

The app averages available campus-day “feels like” values and selects one of five outfit bands: hot, warm, mild, cool, or cold.

Rain, UV, heat, wind, winter precipitation, thunder, and meaningful later changes are evaluated independently, so reminders appear only when their rule applies.

04

Privacy

Location permission is requested only after you choose “Use my location.”

Saved on this device

  • The most recent selected place and the coordinates needed to reload its forecast.
  • When you use device location, exact coordinates are used only for the immediate request and rounded to two decimal places before local storage.
  • Your outfit choices for that place.

Not saved by this app

This app does not save IP addresses or search history. There are no accounts, analytics, or cross-device tracking.

06

Limitations

This is general clothing guidance, not a safety alert or medical recommendation. Comfort varies, forecasts change, and official alerts plus appropriate professional advice take priority.
